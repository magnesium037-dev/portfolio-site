(About Northline)

Northline is an independent landscape and retreat design studio, creating places to pause. We work with wild landscapes, local makers and the people who know each place best, shaping stays deeply connected to where they are.

↗
“A place should feel as though it has always belonged.”

(Philosophy)

Every site asks a different question. We begin with its light, its seasons, its materials, and the ways people might gather there.

(01)

Read
the land

We pay attention to the contours, weather and existing life of a place before drawing a line.

(02)

Build
lightly

Materials, form and footprint are chosen to sit carefully within the landscape and last with it.

(03)

Make room
to gather

A place becomes meaningful through the daily rituals and shared moments it makes possible.

(04)

Grow with
the seasons

We design for changing light, changing needs and the slow beauty that arrives over time.

(The process)

We work with local makers, ecologists and hosts to turn careful observation into generous places for people to reconnect with nature.
